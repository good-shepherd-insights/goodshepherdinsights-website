**Effective Date: July 28, 2026**

Good Shepherd Insights, LLC ("GSI," "we," "us," or "our") is a U.S.-based church technology consulting and fractional CTO business. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information through the Good Shepherd Insights, LLC website, including contact forms, newsletter signup, analytics, and related website interactions (the "Website").

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Website and related website communications. It does not replace any written consulting agreement, statement of work, data processing addendum, nondisclosure agreement, or other contract between GSI and a client church, ministry, vendor, or business partner.

If GSI receives or handles church operational data, member data, donor data, staff data, vendor records, cybersecurity findings, system inventories, or other client-provided information as part of a consulting engagement, that information is governed by the applicable engagement documents and professional obligations, not solely by this Website Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, information collected automatically when you use the Website, and information received from service providers that help operate the Website.

### Information You Provide

When you use the Website contact form, we may collect:

- Full name.
- Church email address.
- Current church size or attendance range.
- Primary technology challenge or inquiry category.
- Message or other information you choose to submit.
- Referral or source information, such as how you heard about GSI.
- Confirmation that you checked an agreement checkbox linked to terms, privacy, or related notices.
- Any other information you voluntarily include in a form, email, or other communication.

When you sign up for the newsletter or other email updates, we may collect:

- Email address.
- Subscription status, signup source, email engagement information, and related Mailchimp-managed list data.

### Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, we or our service providers may collect technical and usage information such as:

- IP address or information derived from an IP address.
- Browser type, device type, operating system, language, screen size, and similar device information.
- Pages viewed, referring pages, links clicked, timestamps, session activity, and general interaction data.
- Approximate location information inferred from technical signals.
- Information collected through cookies, local storage, scripts, pixels, log files, or similar technologies.

The Website uses Umami analytics to help us understand Website traffic and use. Depending on the applicable configuration, Umami and related hosting or network services may process technical information such as page URLs, referrers, browser and device data, timestamps, and IP-address-derived information.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

- Respond to inquiries and contact requests.
- Evaluate whether GSI services may fit a church's needs.
- Schedule conversations, prepare follow-up, and manage prospective client communications.
- Send newsletters, updates, resources, or marketing emails where permitted.
- Operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Website.
- Understand Website performance, content engagement, referral sources, and user navigation patterns.
- Detect, prevent, and investigate spam, abuse, fraud, security incidents, or unauthorized activity.
- Maintain business records and administer our relationships with Website visitors, prospective clients, clients, and service providers.
- Comply with legal, regulatory, contractual, accounting, tax, and risk-management obligations.
- Enforce agreements, protect rights, and respond to lawful requests or legal process.

## 4. How We Share Information

We may share information when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including with:

- **Contact form providers.** The Website uses FormSubmit to transmit contact form submissions to GSI. Information submitted through the contact form may be processed by FormSubmit and delivered to GSI by email or related systems.
- **Email and newsletter providers.** The Website uses Mailchimp for newsletter signup and email list management. Mailchimp may process subscription information, email delivery data, and email engagement data.
- **Analytics providers.** The Website uses Umami analytics to measure Website traffic and engagement.
- **Website hosting, infrastructure, security, and operations providers.** We may use hosting, deployment, DNS, security, email, storage, logging, collaboration, or business operations providers that process information on our behalf.
- **Professional advisors.** We may disclose information to attorneys, accountants, insurers, consultants, auditors, or similar advisors when needed for business, legal, compliance, or risk-management purposes.
- **Legal and safety recipients.** We may disclose information if we believe disclosure is required by law, legal process, government request, court order, or is necessary to protect rights, safety, security, property, or the integrity of our services.
- **Business transaction parties.** If GSI is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, information may be disclosed or transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate protections where required.
- **With your direction or consent.** We may share information when you direct us to do so or when you otherwise consent.

We do not make a blanket claim that information is never shared. We share information with service providers and other recipients as described above. Good Shepherd Insights, LLC does not sell personal information submitted through the Website.

## 5. Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

The Website may use cookies, local storage, scripts, log files, and similar technologies to operate the Website, remember technical preferences, measure Website use, diagnose errors, secure the Website, and understand how visitors interact with content.

Umami analytics helps us assess page views, referrers, events, browser and device characteristics, and related usage trends. Analytics information is used to improve Website content and performance. Browser settings, privacy extensions, or network controls may limit some tracking technologies, but they may not block every form of technical logging or service-provider processing.

## 6. Marketing Emails

If you subscribe to GSI emails, we may send newsletters, resources, event notices, service updates, or other marketing communications. You can unsubscribe by using the unsubscribe link in an email or by contacting us at the privacy contact listed below.

Even if you unsubscribe from marketing emails, we may still send non-marketing communications, such as responses to inquiries, scheduling messages, service-related communications, legal notices, or administrative records related to a business relationship.

## 7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including responding to inquiries, managing prospective and active relationships, maintaining business records, operating and securing the Website, complying with legal obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the context of collection, the sensitivity of the information, legal or contractual requirements, and operational needs. For example, contact form submissions may be retained in email or business systems, newsletter subscription information may remain in Mailchimp unless removed or unsubscribed, and analytics or technical logs may be retained according to provider or system settings.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information. However, no website, email system, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid submitting sensitive church member information, donor records, credentials, payment card information, health information, or other confidential operational data through the public Website contact form unless GSI has provided an appropriate secure channel for that purpose.

## 9. State Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and whether GSI is subject to a particular state privacy law, you may have privacy rights regarding personal information. These rights may include the right to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, opt-out of certain processing, or information about categories of personal information collected, disclosed, sold, or shared.

Many U.S. state privacy laws apply only to businesses that meet specific thresholds, such as revenue levels, numbers of consumers or households whose information is processed, or percentages of revenue derived from selling personal information. GSI may not meet all such thresholds in every jurisdiction. Where a state privacy law applies to GSI, we will respond to rights requests as required by that law.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your identity and authority before fulfilling a request. Authorized agents may submit requests where permitted by applicable law, subject to verification requirements. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

## 10. International Visitors

GSI is based in the United States, and the Website is intended primarily for U.S. visitors and U.S.-based churches and ministries. If you access the Website from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where GSI or its service providers operate. These locations may have privacy laws different from those in your jurisdiction.

## 11. Children's Privacy

The Website is intended for adults acting on behalf of churches, ministries, organizations, vendors, or professional interests. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Website. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Website, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

## 12. Client and Church Operational Data Boundary

The Website contact form should be used for general inquiries, consultation requests, and non-sensitive communications. It should not be used to transmit church member directories, donor histories, giving records, counseling information, pastoral care information, staff HR information, credentials, network diagrams, incident reports, regulated data, or other confidential church operational data.

When GSI works with a client church or ministry, the handling of operational data should be addressed in the applicable agreement, statement of work, security process, confidentiality terms, data processing terms, or mutually approved secure channel. This Website Privacy Policy describes Website data practices and does not by itself define all obligations for consulting engagement data.

## 13. Third-Party Websites and Services

The Website may link to third-party websites, platforms, articles, embedded services, or provider-managed pages. This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites or services that GSI does not control. Third parties process information according to their own privacy notices and terms.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be identified by an updated effective date. Material changes may be communicated through the Website or other appropriate means, depending on the nature of the change and applicable legal requirements. Your continued use of the Website after an updated Privacy Policy is posted means the updated policy applies to information collected after the effective date, subject to applicable law.
